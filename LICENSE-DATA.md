available under CC BY-SA 3.0. The pages used for each quest are linked in the `sources` fields of the data files and in the
application (open a quest, then SOURCES).

## Changes made

The wiki text was read, summarised and reorganised into structured data (prerequisites, cutoffs, mappings to quest journal
files). This work was done with AI assistance (see `NOTICE.txt`). The result may contain errors or omissions and has not
been fully verified against the game.

## Other sources

Facts were also cross-checked against public walkthroughs (Gamer Guides, Fextralife, ConsolePulse, TheGamer, GameBanshee).
Those sites are not licensed for reuse and none of their text is redistributed as such; each quest entry lists the
pages consulted. If you are the owner of a consulted page and want a reference changed or removed, please open an issue.
